# Step 5: Home Assistant

Part of [the editor in Home Assistant plan](00-plan.md).

## Steps

1. `src/editor/ha.js` → `dist/lightwell-card-editor.js`: `lightwell-card-editor` (`setConfig`, `hass`,
   `config-changed`), the shared editor in the HA shell, opening in Build.
2. The card: `getConfigElement` (the loader: its own address, then jsDelivr), `getStubConfig` (`src/stub.js`).
3. `home_url` homes: what the card is, Edit here (into `home:`), or the standalone editor with the file to download.
4. The Release workflow uploads the card first, then the HA editor's bundle.
5. A development build on the user's HA: the card registered under a development tag, both files in
   `/config/www/lightwell-dev/`, a test dashboard.

## Verification

- Tests: the stub passes `defineHome`; the loader's address from a stack line.
- In HA: as in the plan's Verification.

Then (2026-10-04), with the user trying it in HA:

- **"Visual editor not supported":** the development card loads its editor (checked as HA loads it: the card imported as
  a module, its editor found next to it by the stack trace, with its query, in 24 ms); the message is the HACS card's
  (0.2.0 has no editor). Found on the way: two regular expressions were bundled with raw non-ASCII characters (an
  accent range, a °), which break the whole editor on a page read as anything but UTF-8; written as escapes now, and
  `scripts/build.mjs` fails on any byte above 0x7F in a bundle.
- **HA's own preview** takes half of its dialog. Hiding it from the editor (reaching into the dialog's shadow DOM) was
  looked at and decided against with the user, as fragile. Designed around it instead: in the HA shell, the two
  sides are one, with one row of tabs (Items, Properties, YAML, Sun and time) under the plan; selecting something
  shows its properties; the tool hint is cut to two lines (the rest in its tooltip); a dismissible tip says that a
  click on the dialog's title makes it larger (HA's own `large` mode, in HA's frontend since 2024).
- **Editing on HA's preview** (the user found two copies of the plan side by side odd, and chose this): the card, when
  HA makes it its preview (`preview`, set by HA's `hui-card`, which rebuilds the preview on every change), offers
  itself on `window` (`lightwell-preview`; again when an editor opens, `lightwell-editor-open`), and keeps an
  `editLayer` the editor gives it over its plan, through its own rebuilds. The editor in the HA shell adopts the newest
  preview: its editing layer (the overlay, the ruler, the name field, with their styles, `OVERLAY_STYLE`) moves into
  the card, its own copy of the card hides, and the card gets HA's own `hass` (so the simulated sun and time and the
  Dark button go, and Alt+click doesn't tap it). Without a preview it keeps its own plan. `tools/editor/ha.html` has a
  stand-in for HA's preview (rebuilt on every change, marked preview); checked there: selecting, the handles, moving,
  the layer following each rebuild. Not tried in HA itself yet.
- **Dragging on HA's preview wasn't reliable** (the user, in HA; more-info opening): the editing layer was inside the
  card's plan, and the card rebuilds its shadow DOM whenever it gets a new home, which the editor gives it on every
  frame of a drag for the live preview; taking the layer out released its pointer capture, so the rest of the drag
  went astray and its release could land on a marker. The layer is now a child of the card's shadow root that its
  rebuilds leave in place (the rest is replaced around it), and while a card has a layer its markers don't act on
  taps. Checked on the test page: 8 rebuilds during a drag, the layer never left; `ref.html` as before, 0 pixels
  differ. (The test page's synthetic events had missed it: they don't go through pointer capture.)
- **Drags landing above and to the right, and keys not reaching the editor** (the user, in HA): the layer was placed
  from on-screen rectangles, which HA's dialog scales while it opens (it zooms in), and nothing places it again
  after (its layout size doesn't change); and the card's host was inline, so the layer was positioned against an odd
  box. The layer is placed in the frame's own pixels now (screen offsets divided by the frame's scale), and the card
  is a positioned block while it carries a layer. Keys: a click on HA's preview focused nothing of the editor's (the
  card has nothing focusable); the layer is focusable now and hands its keys to the editor, keeping them from HA.
  Checked on the test page: aligned to the drawing exactly, also when measured at 80 %; Delete from the layer deleted,
  and didn't reach the page.
- **The preview in HA's large mode** (the user: a large black emptiness beside it): HA keeps its preview card to
  500 px, also in large mode. The card, while edited on, widened itself to its column (`_fitHosted`, reading the
  column's width, changing nothing of HA's); checked on the test page (478 → 678 px and back) and in HA (632 px). The
  user then preferred HA's 500 px, with the room beside it used for the tools: [step 8](08-layout.md) undoes the
  widening.
- **Drags in HA itself** (tried there with the user signed in, with trusted mouse events): during a drag the overlay
  collapsed to 0 × 0 at the corner, as HA's `ha-card` lays out what's in it a moment after the card rebuilds, and the
  overlay was placed in between. `_place` now leaves the overlay as it is while the drawing has no size, and places it
  again on the next frame. The user confirmed in HA (2026-10-04): **dragging works**.

State on 2026-10-05: built, and tried in HA by the user. Selecting and dragging on HA's preview work; the keyboard on
the preview is still unreliable, and the layout is reworked, in [step 8](08-layout.md). 114 tests pass. The development
card, its resource and the "Lightwell test" dashboard are still on the user's HA, for the next round.
